### Formula requirements

Use safe formulas that handle:
- Empty properties.
- Incomplete records.
- Zero denominators.
- Open trades.
- Missing exit prices.
- Different reporting currencies.

Never treat missing data as zero without explicitly defining that behavior.

Use consistent definitions for all calculated metrics.

For profit factor, use gross winning P&L divided by the absolute value of gross losing P&L.

For drawdown, use chronological cumulative P&L and a running peak, not simply the largest individual loss.

If Notion cannot calculate a metric accurately through native formulas, document the limitation and provide a reliable alternative.
